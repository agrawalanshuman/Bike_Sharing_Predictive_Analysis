Our ISOM 835 team, [NAMES TO FILL IN], with Canvas team name TeamName, proposes “Predicting Next-Day Hourly Bike-Sharing Demand Using Python and Machine Learning”: one observation is one system-wide hour, the target is cnt (total completed rentals), and at 11:59 PM each day we will forecast each of the next calendar day’s 24 hourly counts. A hypothetical bike-sharing operations manager currently plans aggregate service capacity and staffing by repeating the same hour’s rentals from the previous week; our project will assess whether leakage-safe forecasts improve that next-day planning decision, without claiming individual station-level predictions. We will use the public UCI Bike Sharing Dataset, containing approximately 17,379 observed hourly Capital Bikeshare records from 2011–2012 (https://archive.ics.uci.edu/dataset/275/bike+sharing+dataset), compare models with a same-hour-last-week baseline using chronological testing and MAE, and explicitly exclude casual as a suspected leakage column because casual rentals are a component of the target.
