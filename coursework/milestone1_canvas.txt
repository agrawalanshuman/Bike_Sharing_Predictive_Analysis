I, Anshuman, participating under the Canvas project name Anshuman, propose Bike Sharing Predictive Analysis, a project forecasting next-day hourly bike-sharing demand: one observation is one system-wide hour, the target is cnt (total completed rentals), and forecasts are issued at 11:59 PM for each of the following calendar day's 24 hourly counts. A hypothetical bike-sharing operations manager currently plans aggregate service capacity and staffing by repeating the same hour's rentals from the previous week; the project will evaluate whether leakage-safe forecasts improve that planning decision, while recognizing that this dataset cannot support individual station-level allocation. The data come from the public UCI Bike Sharing Dataset, containing approximately 17,379 observed hourly Capital Bikeshare records from 2011–2012 (https://archive.ics.uci.edu/dataset/275/bike+sharing+dataset). I will compare chronological model evaluations against the same-hour-last-week baseline using MAE and exclude casual as a suspected leakage column because it contributes directly to the target.
