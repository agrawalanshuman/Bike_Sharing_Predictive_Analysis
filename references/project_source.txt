 The brief, in one paragraph 
 
 Find one question about the future that an organization — your employer, a business you know, a nonprofit, a team — answers today by guesswork or a spreadsheet: who churns, who defaults, how many, how much, which ones. Build one predictive model in Python that answers it: a leak-proof pipeline, an honest validation, a baseline beaten. Then do the part most projects skip — turn the scores into a decision with a number on it , and show why the decision-maker should trust it: why the model says what it says, how sure it is, and where it fails. That last translation — from probability to decision to trust — is the skill this course exists to teach.
 
 
 
 
 

 
 
 
 
 
 
 Beat 1 
 A real question 
 Unit, target, horizon, and a decision someone actually owns and makes badly today. 
 
 
 
 Beat 2 
 Honest data 
 Wrangled, understood, leaks removed — with the split before any fitted transformer. 
 
 
 
 Beat 3 
 A validated model 
 The right metric on a held-out set that mirrors production, beating the obvious baseline. 
 
 
 
 Beat 4 
 A decision with a number 
 A threshold, a list, or a reorder point — and what it is worth, estimated honestly. 
 
 
 
 Beat 5 
 A trust story 
 Why (SHAP), how sure (calibration, conformal), where it fails (slices), and what to monitor.
